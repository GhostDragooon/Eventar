# Eventar

A CPD/CME compliance platform for regulated professions in Hong Kong. Practitioners attend accredited events; Eventar records attendance and issues tamper-evident credit that their regulator will accept.

> Status: Stage 10 (2026 dual-track) — see `docs/plans/STAGES.md` and `docs/plans/PROJECT_STATE.md` for the current phase. Pivoted 2026-07-03 from an internal workshop manager; the event/attendance/QR-checkin loop from that product carries forward as the CPD platform's backbone.
