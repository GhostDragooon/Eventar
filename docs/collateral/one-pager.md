**What it is.** Eventar is a Hong Kong platform for running accredited CPD/CME events and issuing tamper-evident credit records. Organisers run the event; attendance is captured at the door; credits post to each practitioner's **CPD Ledger** — a record their accrediting body can verify independently.

## The loop

```
Publish accredited event  →  Practitioners register (2 minutes)
        ↓                                 ↓
Body confirms points          Personal QR pass by email
        ↓                                 ↓
   At the door: QR scan / manual code — per person, timestamped
        ↓
Credits post to the CPD Ledger  →  visible in the practitioner's wallet
        ↓
Audit-ready evidence for the accrediting body, on demand
```

## Why the CPD Ledger is different

- **Attendance is captured as a fact, not claimed as a memory.** Credits come from a timestamped, per-person check-in at the event — not a self-reported form vetted weeks later by a third party.
- **The record cannot be silently altered.** Every entry is chained to the one before it; corrections are new entries. Not even our own operators can edit or delete a posted credit — the database refuses at the permission layer.
- **Your body can check the math.** Any record can be verified against the chain independently — trust the mathematics, not the vendor.
