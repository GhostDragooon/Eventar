## Motion spec for the builder (stage 5)
Same language as UX-01 and UX-FU1: current-place change is a 150 ms colour fade on the row (the product's `.nav-item` already does 180 ms on the hero curve; keep it), no travel, never animate height or width, buttons keep `active:translate-y-px`, nothing on a timer, everything driven by the action's result. Sheet and drawer: 200 ms opacity plus a short slide from their edge, exit faster than enter, focus moves into the sheet and returns to its trigger on close, Escape closes. Reduced motion: slide removed, fade kept.
