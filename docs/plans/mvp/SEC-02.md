# SEC-02 authenticated cannot UPDATE events.id, created_by, created_at, updated_at or deleted_at
STATUS: DONE locally. Found by the UPD-01 backtest; no separate review round (a 10-line grant change, proven by the real edit RPC, a real PATCH and the full RLS suite). Hosted push: see SEC-01.md "Hosted".
Origin: UPD-01 backtest F2 [X]: a colleague (any active staff member of the event's organisation) PATCHed `created_by` of a PUBLISHED event to their own staff id (204, no audit row). That defeats the creator rule (Ivan's ruling 2, 2026-10-23) and falsifies who made an event; `id` and `deleted_at` were writable too.
Changed: `supabase/migrations/20261023160000_events_authenticated_cannot_update_identity_columns.sql` (new), `supabase/seed.sql`, `tests/rls/audited_table_writes.rls.test.ts`, `tests/rls/seed_grant_posture.rls.test.ts`.
What it does: revokes table-level UPDATE on `events` from anon and authenticated (which clears every column grant; a column-level REVOKE alone no-ops against a table grant) and grants the column list back without the five. `status`, `organisation_id`, `published_at`, `accrediting_body_id`, `cpd_hours` stay definer-only as before; INSERT keeps `created_by` (written at insert only); `service_role` untouched. A self-check raises, and the push rolls back, if a locked column is still writable or an ordinary one is not. Nothing needs the five [R, X]: `update_event_with_blocks` never sets them, a soft delete goes through a definer function, and `updated_at` is a trigger assignment (not a column privilege).
