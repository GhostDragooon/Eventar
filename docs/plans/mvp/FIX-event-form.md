## Not fixed

- 5b Timezone line contradiction: left as is. The copy is accurate to the code: before a venue is picked the typed times ARE read in the browser's zone (`new Date(\`${date}T${hh:mm}:00\`)`, DateTimeSection `VenueTimeLine` says so), and `Timezone follows the venue` describes how they are DISPLAYED. The proposed `Times use the venue's timezone once you pick a venue` would be false: typed times never switch to the venue zone. A real fix is a product decision (interpret typed times in the venue zone), not a copy change.
- The window in finding 1's envelope sentence is the typed event window (12h, same as the pickers) with no zone suffix: agenda times and event times are both read in the browser zone, so adding the venue zone would label them with the wrong clock.
- Dialog focus lands on its close X (the shared `Dialog` focuses the first field, else X), not on `Keep editing`. Shared component; not touched.
- The agenda highlight stays forced until the next Save click; blocks added after a refused save show red until then. Accepted (simplest).
