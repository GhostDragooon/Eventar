## Worked well
- Two saves in a row without reloading made no duplicates [X]. Added a third block, saved ("Draft saved. Review and publish when you are ready." / "Saved."), changed its title, saved again, reloaded: exactly three blocks in the form. The database agreed: three rows, the two seeded blocks still carrying my fixture ids (so they were updated, not recreated) and one new row for the third block, title the latest one.
- The survey-answer refusal is in plain words with the block's title in it, and it changed nothing in the database: Panel, its times and its survey reference were all still there afterwards [X].
- A published event cannot be edited from the UI: the form is simply not offered [X]. The agenda for it is shown read-only.
- The details page of the published event still shows its registration section: "Close registration on (optional)" with a Save button, disabled until a value is entered [X].

## Not reached (and why)
- Remove "Opening", save, reload: my Remove click ran 800 ms after the page loaded and did nothing (page probably not hydrated); a later run with a 1000 ms wait worked, but the budget was gone. The same early run's save of an unedited form gave no notice at all, which I could not tell apart from the same timing problem, so I do not claim it as a finding.
- Registration-close editor on the published event: inconclusive. I set a value by script and pressed Save; the page reloaded under the script, and afterwards `registration_close_at` was still NULL and the row's `updated_at` unchanged. The editor's real date control was not found by my selectors (no button named "Registration closes"), so this may be my harness and not the product. Needs one manual or curl try.
- Empty block title on its own, out-of-window block save, double-click Save: only a new block with no title and no times was tried (U4's first message); double-click not run.
- The copy "Your changes were saved, but the agenda could not be read back. Reload this page before saving again.": needs a read-back failure, which the UI cannot cause.
- The screen position of the refusal message (inside the Agenda section, near Save, or at the top): not checked; no screenshot taken.
- Phone width and keyboard-only use: not tested.
