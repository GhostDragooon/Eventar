Decision 1 — Status pills (3 new, 3 existing for comparison)

Existing — unchanged

Drafted Registering Completed

(Same as today. "Registering" relabels the existing "Published" — no style change.)

⚖️ Choose one — LIVE pill

A · Solid primary + white dot

Live

Loud, hard attention pull. Pulses against the indigo. Distinct from Registering. Risk: in a Live-heavy section the row of solid indigo pills could overwhelm.

B · Container + pulse dot

Live

Quieter. Same fill as Registering, the dot does the work. Risk: at a glance Registering and Live look similar — pulse motion the only differentiator.

C · Tertiary-fixed-dim + dot

Live

Warm-orange (tertiary palette) — distinct from all other pills, "happening right now" energy. Risk: orange may read as warning/error in a serious internal tool.

⚖️ Choose one — UPCOMING pill (registration closed, awaiting start)

A · Surface-container-highest (frozen)

Upcoming

Muted grey — "locked in, waiting." Reads as inert. Distinct from Drafted (lighter grey) and Registering (indigo). Conservative.

B · Primary-fixed (cool-blue waiting)

Upcoming

Pale-indigo — within the brand palette, signals "primary-adjacent." Visually quieter than Registering, same family. Risk: too close to Registering at a glance.

C · Tertiary-container/10 (warm waiting)

Upcoming

Warm-brown subtle — third color register beyond primary/grey. My original proposal in the design doc. Risk: tertiary in this palette reads brown-orange, may feel out of place in an indigo-dominant UI.

Single proposal — CANCELLED pill (rare; fixes a quiet fallback bug)

Cancelled

Error-container at 30% opacity. Distinct from all other pills, restrained intensity. Not part of lifecycle progression — only shown if explicitly set.
